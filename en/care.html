Long-term care needs

Where help was needed

In 2024, 902 respondents needed help. Of these, 413 needed basic help and 489 needed instrumental help only (7.06% and 9.86% of all adults aged 65+, weighted). Among the 902, 204 reported no helper (25.98% weighted).

How this is measured

The eight common items cover basic self-care (dressing, bathing, eating, toileting) and instrumental activities of daily living (meals, shopping, finances, medication). Help with any item indicates functional care need. Separate questions cover insurance eligibility and use.

2024 questionnaire · pp. 104–107, C201–C220, and p. 112, C318–C325

People needing help on any of the 17 Korean items can name up to five helpers, including relatives, volunteers and paid carers. Our no-helper estimate uses the eight-item need group. The roster does not assess whether help with each task was adequate.

Help needs by number of diagnosed conditions

In 2024, 12.18% of those reporting none of the eight diagnoses still needed help with an activity, compared with 25.78% of those reporting at least three. These estimates do not adjust for differences in age composition.
