Assessment history and care needs

Share of those needing help with no disability assessment history

Of the 902 respondents who needed help with at least one of eight activities in 2024, 752 had no reported history of being assessed by a physician as having a disability. Their weighted share was 83.8%.

Basis for comparison

How do older adults in Korea and China differ?

Our first question asks how disability, chronic disease burden, cognition and long-term care needs differ between Korea and China. Both surveys collected data in 2018. Estimates here use Korean individual-level data. Chinese questionnaires and codebooks establish candidate measures for comparison. The 2024 results describe Korea more recently.
