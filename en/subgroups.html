Health and care by sex, age and socioeconomic position

Of 4,352 respondents aged 65+ in 2018, 3,063 (70.4%) also participated in 2024. Among the 4,504 respondents aged 65+ in 2024, 1,286 were interviewed below age 65 in 2018 and 155 had no 2018 main interview. The 2024 wave gives a recent Korean profile. The 2018 wave aligns with the Chinese CHARLS survey year.

How does the income gap vary with age?

In 2024, 25.4% of the lowest income quartile and 12.8% of the highest needed help with at least one of eight activities. The corresponding shares were 12.6% and 9.2% at ages 65–74, and 22.6% and 20.0% at ages 75–84. At age 85+ the ordering reversed (50.2% and 62.1%), with 71 valid respondents in the highest quartile. The overall difference does not apply uniformly across ages or establish an income effect.

Change among the same respondents

Of the 2018 sample, 1,289 were absent in 2024. Exit interviews in waves 8–10 recorded 978 deaths. The status of the other 311 is unresolved. Re-observation was 60.8% in the lowest 2018 income quartile and 76.5% in the highest. The table gives unweighted counts for respondents observed at both endpoints.

Methods and downloads

Weights and counts Estimates use each wave’s integrated individual cross-sectional weight (wgt_ac). Counts are unweighted. Each estimate uses valid responses to that indicator.

Income quartiles Prior-year household income excludes real-estate and financial income. Divide by the square root of household size at interview. Compute weighted quartiles separately among eligible respondents with known income in each wave. Ties at a cutoff enter the lower quartile.

Missing income Exclude missing income only in income-based tables. The overall, sex, age and education tables retain these respondents. Income cutoffs are in KRW 10,000 per year.

Reading indicators The four basic activities are dressing, bathing, eating and toileting. The eight common activities also include preparing meals, taking medication, shopping and managing money. Any partial or full assistance meets the threshold.

Conditions and cognition Multimorbidity means at least two of eight physician-diagnosed physical conditions. K-MMSE-2 is a 0–30 score. We report the 2024 mean among valid tests. The 2018 scores are unavailable in this release.

Linked respondents Paired transitions use respondents aged 65+ and valid for the indicator at both endpoints. Counts and percentages are unweighted. Intermediate changes are unobserved.

Group differences Descriptive estimates are unadjusted. Residual differences in age may persist within age bands. The samples overlap and should not be treated as independent.
